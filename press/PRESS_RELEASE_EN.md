NOIRIX launches an online store for modest fashion and accessories in the Gulf, with prices from AED 19

NOIRIX has launched noirix.shop, an online store offering abayas, kaftans, dresses, bags, accessories and home pieces in a calm, minimal style, with clear prices starting at AED 19.

The store runs in Arabic and English and shows prices in the visitor's local currency, with free delivery on orders above a set amount in each country. New pieces are added every day after each product is reviewed by hand: photos, colours, sizes and fit for women in the Gulf.

"We wanted a store where a woman finds an elegant, modest piece at a clear price, with no fake discounts and no pressure to buy," said Musa Elsharif, founder of NOIRIX. "Every piece is reviewed before it appears in the store."
